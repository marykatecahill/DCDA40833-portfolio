Visualization Critique

Visualization

Critique

Information Resolution

Your analysis of how comprehensive and complete the visualization is. Discuss whether the visualization provides sufficient detail, appropriate aggregation, and supports the viewer's task.

Visual Encoding and Clarity

Assess the choice of visual encodings (color, size, position) and their effectiveness. Note any misleading scales, color issues, or clutter that reduce readability.

Context and Annotation

Evaluate whether the visualization provides necessary context: clear labels, units, legends, and annotations to prevent misinterpretation.

Conclusion

Summarize the overall effectiveness and integrity of the visualization, and provide actionable suggestions for improvement.
