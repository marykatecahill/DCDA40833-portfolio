About Me

Who I am: My name is Mary Kate Cahill, and I am a Strategic Communications major with a minor in Digital Culture and Data Analytics (DCDA). I am currently a senior and plan to graduate in May 2026. I am interested in how people, brands, and technology connect in today’s digital world. In my classes, I have worked on projects that mix creativity with planning, like digital stories, research projects, and social media ideas. I enjoy working with others and sharing ideas to make messages clearer and more meaningful. I see myself as someone who cares about both the people behind the message and the tools used to share it. I want to keep building skills that will help me grow and adapt in different jobs after I graduate..

My interests: I am passionate about digital media and how it shapes almost every part of business today. I like learning how companies use social media, websites, and data to reach people and tell their stories. I chose the DCDA minor because it lets me learn both creative and technical skills at the same time. I enjoy seeing how numbers and feedback can show what people like, what they click on, and what keeps their attention. This helps businesses make better choices and improve how they connect with their audience. I am especially interested in how digital tools can make messages clearer, more personal, and more engaging for different groups of people.

What I hope to learn: I hope to become more comfortable and confident with coding. Right now, it feels hard and sometimes confusing, but I know it is an important skill to have. I want to learn how to write simple code, fix small problems, and understand what is happening behind the scenes on websites and digital tools. This will help me feel more independent and less nervous when working with technology. I am excited to learn how coding can help with things like looking at data, building simple websites, and creating online projects. My goal is not to be a full-time programmer, but to feel confident using coding as a helpful skill in my future career!
